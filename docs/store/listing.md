**App name:** W-Calc — Scientific Calculator

**Short description (80 chars max):**
The scientific calculator you use in class — on your phone, keys and all.

**Full description:**

Practise with a calculator that looks and works like the one in your school bag.

W-Calc gives you nine scientific calculators to choose from:
• Two-line models with the classic ten-digit result line
• Textbook-display models that show fractions, roots and powers as written — with exact answers
  like 7/6, 2√3 + √6 or π/6
• Multi-line models that keep earlier calculations on screen, with 2nd / 2ndF keys

Everything works: fractions, statistics and regression, equations up to four unknowns, matrices up
to 4×4, vectors, complex numbers, base-n and logic, integration, differentiation, sums, CALC and
SOLVE, 40 physical constants and 40 unit conversions.

Plus:
• Graphs — tap the chart icon to see your function, equation or data; trace points, find roots
  and intersections
• A searchable manual with worked examples for the model you use
• Themes and key finishes (classic, glossy, matte, rubber, metallic and more)
• Full-screen focus mode, key vibration, works offline, no ads, no tracking

Made for learning and practice. Most exams do not allow phones — take your approved calculator
into the exam room.

Compatible with the key sequences of popular MS-series and ES PLUS-series scientific calculators.
W-Calc is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any
calculator manufacturer. Product names belong to their owners.
