# W-Calc privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

W-Calc does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal data.

- **No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.**
- **Works offline.** The app does not send data over the network.
- **Stored on your device only:** your settings (model, theme, finish, vibration, tours seen),
  calculator memories and data, calculation history (unless you switch saving off) and graphs. They stay in the app's private storage and
  are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
- **Links:** "Buy me a coffee" and "Source code" open your web browser (Ko-fi, GitHub); W-Calc
  itself sends nothing.
- **Permissions:** vibration only (for key presses). No camera, microphone, location, contacts or
  storage access.
- **Children:** W-Calc is suitable for students of all ages and collects nothing from anyone.

Questions: contact the developer through the store listing.
