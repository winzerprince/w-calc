Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The calculator

W-Calc (Android app, itch.io version and this website) does not ask for an account and does not collect, sell or share personal data. Your settings, calculator memories, calculation history and graphs are stored only in your device or browser storage. Clearing the app data or your browser's site data removes them.

Advertising on this website

This website (w-calc.0xwinzer.com) may show ads served by Google AdSense to keep W-Calc free. Google and its partners use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

The Android app and the itch.io version contain no ads.

Hosting

The website is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against abuse. W-Calc itself keeps no server logs.

Links

“Buy me a coffee”, “Source code” and download buttons open Ko-fi, GitHub or itch.io, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

W-Calc is made for students. It collects nothing from anyone.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/winzerprince/w-calc.
