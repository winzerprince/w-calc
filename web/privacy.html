Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Your data

W-Calc (Android app, itch.io version and this website) does not ask for an account and does not collect, sell or share personal data — from anyone. Your settings, calculator memories, calculation history and graphs stay in your device or browser storage; clearing the app data or your browser's site data removes them. Like any website, the servers that deliver this one handle basic technical data (such as IP addresses) only to keep it running securely. Links to Ko-fi, GitHub or itch.io follow those sites' own policies.

Advertising on this website

This website (w-calc.0xwinzer.com) may show ads served by Google AdSense to keep W-Calc free. Google and its partners use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

The Android app and the itch.io version contain no ads.
